## Review rule

The ten `review` candidates remain in the P0 queue. They are not rejected as restaurants; they are rejected **for immediate automated dish-first intake** until a stable current first-party dish list can be proven. No secondary guide menu is promoted to canonical source authority merely to make a candidate pass.
